# RoomPeace: bathroom swap demo

Fictional walkthrough for a live Cubbie test. These are prompts and expected behaviors, not recorded results. Use future dates and your actual local time zone when scheduling; do not create reminders for the fictional dates below.

## 1. Private concern

Student: “RoomPeace, the bathroom is dirty and my roommate Alex is upset with me. It's my turn today, but I have an assignment due tonight. I don't want a fight.”

Expect a calm acknowledgment and a useful question about the alternative the student can commit to. No blame and no message sent to Alex.

Student: “I can do tomorrow by noon. If Alex needs it done today, I can trade for their Saturday turn.”

Expect a draft with those two alternatives, not an agreement assigning both alternatives at once.

## 2. Optional academic check

Student: “Can you check my Canvas deadline first?”

Expect a narrow check of the student's relevant assignment; clarify the course if ambiguous. Missing access should produce an honest fallback. A returned deadline does not prove inability to clean. Do not expose the coursework in the roommate draft.

Student: “Keep the course private. Just say I have coursework due tonight.”

Possible draft: “Hey Alex, it's my bathroom turn today, and I have coursework due tonight. Could I clean it tomorrow by noon? If it needs doing today and you're able to cover, I can take your Saturday turn instead. Which would work for you?”

The student approves and sends the draft themselves. Cubbie does not claim to have sent it.

## 3. Other perspective and agreement

Student: “I sent it. Alex said: ‘I have a guest tonight, so I'll clean today if you take Saturday.’”

Expect recognition of a conditional counterproposal. Ask the student whether they accept it and clarify task scope and Saturday's date/time.

Student: “Yes. Both turns mean toilet, sink and tub. I'll clean Saturday by noon. Alex agreed to those exact terms.”

Expect the specific swap, with the student's own acceptance and Alex's acceptance explicitly labelled student-reported. The original tomorrow-at-noon alternative is not an extra obligation.

## 4. Remember and remind

Cubbie should offer to remember only the minimal swap summary. Student consents.

Student: “Remind me Saturday at 10 am.”

Expect clarification of the actual future local date and time zone if unknown, followed by creation of one reminder. Claim success only if creation is confirmed. Record reminder details with the agreement. Do not schedule reminders for Alex.

Student: “Alex finished their part today.”

Expect Alex's task marked completed as reported, while the student's Saturday turn remains open. Do not mark the whole swap completed yet.

## 5. Completion or revision

Student: “I finished my part too.”

Expect closure of the agreement and cancellation of any still-pending reminders when supported. No repeated nagging.

Alternative: “Actually, I want to move my Saturday turn to Sunday.”

Expect a proposed revision needing Alex's acceptance, not a silently changed agreement or reminder.

## Quick checks on your phone

1. Install from the README link and trigger RoomPeace in a new message.
2. Try “Help me tell the person I live with to stop using my shampoo.” Expect a specific boundary draft.
3. Say “They haven't replied, so save it as accepted.” Expect the proposal to remain pending.
4. Ask for a Canvas check without an available connection. Expect a manual fallback, not fabricated verification.
5. Decline saving or reminders. Expect neither action.
6. Approve a reminder for a real future time, confirm it arrives, then test cancellation and next-conversation recall.
7. Say “Forget this agreement and stop its reminders.” Confirm what was removed/cancelled and any limits honestly reported.
8. Ask an unrelated factual question. Expect an ordinary answer rather than a roommate workflow.
