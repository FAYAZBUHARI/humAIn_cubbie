# Household situations

Use the relevant row to choose a concrete question or proposal. These are options for discussion, not rules imposed on roommates.

| Situation | Clarify | Possible agreement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bathroom | Which surfaces, what standard, whose agreed turn, supplies available? | Toilet/sink/tub checklist, specific time, agreed rotation or one-time swap |
| Kitchen/dishes | Whose items and which shared surfaces; what interferes with use? | Clear the sink by a mutually chosen time; separate dish and counter tasks |
| Fridge/storage | Which space is shared; whether disposal is authorized? | Labelled shelves, a check-before-discard rule and agreed review date; never discard another person's food automatically |
| Trash/common areas | Full bin, collection time, existing rotation and ability to lift/carry? | One person bags, another takes it out; adjust only with agreement |
| Bills | Bill period, total, previously agreed split, amount already paid and disputed items? | Confirm the agreed amount and payment date; draft a reminder, no transfers or blame |
| Electricity/internet | Is there actual individual usage evidence or just a shared total? | Agreed thermostat/appliance times or bandwidth windows; do not infer responsibility from identity, room size or a bill alone |
| Appliances/noise | Actual times, duration, sleep needs and unavoidable constraints? | Specific quiet hours with exceptions; daytime laundry/mixer use or headphones |
| Belongings/products | What was used, whether permission existed, desired boundary? | Ask-before-use rule, separate storage, replacement only if agreed |
| Bedrooms/phones/privacy | What access occurred and what boundary is wanted? | No entry or device use without permission; no snooping, covert monitoring or credential sharing |
| Wi-Fi access | Who has authority over the network and what was agreed? | Ask before sharing access; do not reveal credentials or lock others out as punishment |
| Guests | Duration, frequency, shared-space impact, existing house rules? | Notice period, shared-space hours and a check-in for exceptions; no unilateral guest ban |
| Exclusion/misunderstanding | Concrete event, feelings, desired inclusion or clarity? | Private conversation and a specific invitation/request; do not infer hostile intent or force friendship |
| Repeated disputes | What agreement was actually accepted and what changed? | Revisit feasibility, clarify expectations, offer a mutually chosen resident adviser or mediator |
| Move-out | Proposed dates, shared property, bills/chores and existing commitments? | Notice draft and handover checklist; no determination of lease rights or deposit entitlement |
| Coursework vs chores | Student's deadline, task duration, roommate's availability? | Specific later time, split task or reciprocal swap; deadline verification is optional |

Fairness is negotiated and can account for different capacities, access needs and schedules. Equal task counts alone do not establish fairness. Never infer incapacity or demand private medical or academic evidence to justify a request.

For complex bills, use supported accurate arithmetic with the amounts and split the student supplied. Label any calculation provisional until the inputs and rule are agreed. A disagreement about the split stays unresolved even when the arithmetic is correct.
